# Storyboard: Break Hub Intro

## Frame 01: Home Page Tour
- **Duration:** 8s
- **Voiceover:** "Welcome to Break Hub — your ultimate retro-future arcade portal for instant gaming action."
- **Visuals:** Dynamic overview of the Break Hub home page interface, glowing neon grid background, spotlight game cards, and retro arcade branding.
- **Shot Sequence:**
  - 0.0s - 2.5s: Fade in from deep space indigo with radiating neon grid lines and glowing portal title card.
  - 2.5s - 5.5s: Smooth slide-in of featured arcade game cards (Starfighter, Knife Thrower, Fruit Merge).
  - 5.5s - 8.0s: Pulse highlight on interactive play buttons and gamer profile badge.
- **Asset Candidates:** capture/extracted/tokens.json

## Frame 02: Starfighter & Knife Thrower Gameplay
- **Duration:** 12s
- **Voiceover:** "Engage in high-velocity space combat against Vader’s capital ship in Starfighter, then test your precision in Cyber Knife Thrower."
- **Visuals:** Fast-paced cuts matching the 80s synth beat: Luke's X-wing firing quad lasers against the towering Star Destroyer boss, followed by knives sticking into spinning cyber wheels.
- **Shot Sequence:**
  - 8.0s - 14.0s: Starfighter combat arena action — X-wing strafing left/right and firing quad lasers against diving enemy scouts and the Vader Command capital ship.
  - 14.0s - 20.0s: Cyber Knife Thrower wheel rotation action — glowing blades tossing and sticking precisely into rotating target sectors with spark burst particles.
- **Asset Candidates:** capture/extracted/asset-descriptions.md

## Frame 03: Leaderboards & Victory
- **Duration:** 10s
- **Voiceover:** "Climb the global high score leaderboards, unlock achievements, and claim your arcade glory. Play. Compete. Repeat."
- **Visuals:** Live leaderboard ranking rows with player avatars, neon badges, final call to action button, and glowing arcade portal summary.
- **Shot Sequence:**
  - 20.0s - 24.5s: Slide-in of global leaderboard rankings table with top gamer tags and high scores.
  - 24.5s - 30.0s: Grand finale title card with "PLAY. COMPETE. REPEAT." badge and glowing neon arcade portal gateway.
- **Asset Candidates:** capture/extracted/tokens.json

## Video direction
- **Palette:** Deep obsidian space background (`#030014`), vibrant neo cyan (`#00F0FF`), neo magenta (`#FF007F`), and arcade yellow (`#FFE600`).
- **Typography:** Heavy Impact headers, bold sans-serif body copy with hard neo-brutalist drop shadows.
- **Motion:** Fast-paced beat-synced cuts, neon pulsing glows, and camera slide transitions.
